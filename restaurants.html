Nothing changes at your register.

  1. They pay as usual

    Cash, card, anything. Nibblr doesn’t touch your register, your POS or your payments.

  2. They tap the tag

    On orders of $10 or more, the diner taps the Nibblr tag by your register with their phone. That’s a punch.

  3. Rewards go on the comp button

    Using a reward, the diner shows your cashier a screen like this one. Your cashier applies it with your comp button. The same tap earns a punch.

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?
Nothing during the 12-week pilot, and we never ask for payment details. After it, we’ll only talk about pricing if we can show Nibblr brought you more business, and it’s your call whether to continue.
Do my customers need an app?
Not to start earning. One tap on the tag with their phone gives them a punch. They’ll need the app to spend punches on a reward.
I tried a loyalty app before. It wasn’t worth it.
Nibblr doesn’t hook into your register, and no payments go through us: diners pay you the usual way and never pre-load money. And it’s shared, so it brings you diners who earn at spots nearby, not just your own regulars. If the pilot doesn’t bring you more business, you walk away owing nothing.
I already have a loyalty program.
Keep it. Nibblr runs alongside your own punch card or Square Loyalty, and doesn’t touch your register.
